# Song list: first 30 bass songs

A starter list for practising in It's MyTabs. Tabs are not included: you add each one yourself (see README-MAC.md, section 3).

## Where to get the files

- **Ultimate Guitar** (GP column): look for results of type **Guitar Pro**. Downloading needs a free account. Upload the `.gp`/`.gp5` file into the app.
- **Songsterr** (SS column): good for viewing and listening in the browser, but it does not give you a file to import. Use it to check a line, or as a backup.
- Prefer tabs with a high rating and many votes. Accuracy of community tabs varies.

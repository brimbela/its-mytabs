# Song list: bass songs to learn

Brief change (22 Oct 2026): finding tabs online is now allowed. This list links straight to the best bass tab found for each song.

## How to use the links

- **GP file** column: an Ultimate Guitar page with a **Download original Guitar Pro tab** button. Log in (free account), download, then in the app click **New Tab** and choose the file. This is the only kind you can import.
- **Bass tab** column: a page to read or play in the browser. Ultimate Guitar text tabs and Songsterr can't be imported. Songsterr is the most accurate for the Portuguese songs: it plays the tab with the original audio.
- In a GP file, pick the bass track in the app (click the track name, bottom left). Some GP files are full-band files where the bass track was not checked yet.
- Tuning: some songs are half a step down (Eb). It is noted below.
